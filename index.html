Investment Rationale

Portfolio
Logic

Reaching 6% on a diversified basis requires deliberate blending. No single sleeve achieves the target — the portfolio is constructed so complementary income sources compound each other toward the goal without excessive concentration risk.

Construction Principle

Why Equal Weighting?

Equal weighting avoids concentration in any single income source. The lower-yielding, higher-quality UK equity and passive positions (3.8–5.1%) are balanced by specialist higher-yielders in credit, infrastructure and Asia (7–9.5%).

Annual rebalancing to equal weights enforces a buy-low discipline — trimming whatever has outperformed and adding to whatever has lagged — and captures mean-reversion in investment trust discounts.
